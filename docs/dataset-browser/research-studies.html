Research studies have separate questions, shortlists, filter views and annotations. Dataset files and paper records are shared. Source permissions must be reviewed for each study’s purpose.
Removing a study: a browser draft is deleted here for good (export it first if you want to keep it). A registered study is removed from the registry with Scripts/register_research_study.py --remove STUDY_ID --apply; its reports and action log stay in docs/research-studies/STUDY_ID/. The default study cannot be removed.
Use an HTTP preview for study switching across pages; file:// browser storage may be isolated per page. Browser drafts are local to this browser/origin. Export a backup and pass it to Claude Code or Codex to register the study with Scripts/register_research_study.py --request /path/to/study.json --apply. Registration grants no data permissions. Imported/exported annotations do not grant licences.
